About Me

Hello! I build products.

I'm Anh Tuan Le, a third-year Software Engineering student at FPT University Hanoi.

I work across two tracks that reinforce each other: full-stack development — React and Node.js over MySQL, with REST APIs, role-based access and real-time features — and business and data analysis, where I handle stakeholder coordination, event operations, sponsorship, budgeting and procurement, and turn the results into reporting people can act on.

That combination comes from building things and then having to run them. I founded the Robotics Club at FPT University Hanoi and helped start its Da Nang and Quy Nhon chapters, co-founded VEX Technology Solutions as CEO & CTO, and founded SkillPASS. Each one meant writing the software and also owning the schedule, the budget and the people around it.

Feel free to explore my projects and experiences, and don't hesitate to reach out! I’m always open to new connections, learning opportunities, and collaborations.
